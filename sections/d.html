How It Works

Three easy steps to secure an unforgettable audio guestbook experience for your event.

1

Choose Your Phone

Browse our exclusive collection of vintage telephones and pick the one that matches your wedding theme.

2

Record & Celebrate

We deliver the phone to your venue. Guests pick up the receiver, hear your greeting, and leave messages.

3

Cherish Forever

After the event, we process and compile all voice messages into a private digital keepsake for you to replay anytime.
